## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Sister Other Paranoia**. This trainer enhances the game's visual-novel progression by providing convenient control over story choices, route access, text progression, and save-state management. It is designed for a choice-driven experience centered on mind reading, emotional manipulation, five motif-based scenarios, and multiple endings rather than traditional exploration or combat.

**Target Audience:** Players who want to replay story branches, explore alternate endings, revisit mind-reading scenes, or reduce repetitive dialogue while preserving the game's narrative focus.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Sister Other Paranoia does not use character movement or real-time exploration. Progress is handled through visual-novel dialogue, interface navigation, motif selection, and story choices. Use the trainer's progression options to move between narrative sections more efficiently.

### Tools & Inventory

The game does not feature a conventional inventory system. Progress is primarily determined by dialogue choices, scenario completion, route state, and ending conditions. Save-state tools are useful before selecting important responses.

### World & Environment

After the prologue, the story branches into five motif-based scenarios: Bat, Caterpillar, Monkey, Ring, and Fox. Each scenario contains four chapters, and completing routes contributes to the game's broader ending structure. The trainer can assist with revisiting or unlocking narrative progression.

### Combat & Weapons

There is no traditional combat system, character leveling, weapon management, or battle encounters. The central gameplay mechanic is psychological choice-making, including the protagonist's ability to read minds and extension emotional vulnerabilities. Trainer functions therefore focus on narrative progression rather than combat statistics.
